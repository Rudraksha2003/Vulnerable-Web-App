What happened?

The app loaded a script from a URL you controlled. That script ran with full access to this page and stole the secret—exactly like a compromised or malicious third-party dependency (e.g. a tampered analytics or feedback library). In practice: allowlist widget URLs, use Subresource Integrity (SRI), or load only from trusted origins you control.
